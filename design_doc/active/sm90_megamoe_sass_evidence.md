# SM90 MegaMoE SASS evidence for CAKE

_H20 evidence note for the DeepSeek-V4-Flash MXFP4 campaign, captured 2026-09-13_

---

## 📋 Scope and provenance

This note records static evidence that CAKE workers may use when evolving the
SM90 MXFP4 MegaMoE kernel. The denominator is fixed at `H=4096`, `IH=2048`,
`E=256`, `topk=6`, `world_size=8`. The FP8 binaries were generated by an
earlier local reproduction of DeepGEMM PR #383[^1] and inspected with CUDA
13.1 `cuobjdump --dump-resource-usage` and `nvdisasm`.

The Aichen `megamoe_nvfp4_dev_m` source is a structural reference for FP4
decode, scale handling, warp roles, and register budgeting.[^2] No matching
NVFP4 or MXFP4 cubin was present in the retained JIT caches, so the instruction
counts below are FP8 denominator evidence, not a claim about MXFP4 codegen.

## 📊 Static resource and instruction evidence

All four inspected Flash-shape FP8 specializations use `REG=168`,
`LOCAL=0`, and `SHARED=1024` bytes of static shared memory. The kernels use
dynamic shared-memory pools in addition to the static figure, so `SHARED=1024`
must not be interpreted as total CTA shared-memory consumption.

| Specialization | Stages | QGMMA body | LDS family | STS family | SYNCS.EXCH | WARPGROUP pairs |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `experts/wave=8, BN=256` | 5 | `4 x 64x128x32` | 6 | 28 | 11 | 1 |
| `experts/wave=8, BN=128` | 8 | `4 x {8,16,32,64}x32` | 68 | 60 | 17 | 4 |
| `experts/wave=64, BN=256` | 4 | `4 x 64x128x32` | 6 | 28 | 9 | 1 |
| `experts/wave=64, BN=128` | 8 | `4 x {8,16,32,64}x32` | 68 | 60 | 17 | 4 |

The counts are static disassembly counts, not executed instruction counts.
They show that the `BN=128` path carries substantially more shared-memory
load/store and synchronization instructions in its compiled body, while the
`BN=256` path expresses one wide QGMMA sequence. Runtime scheduling, predicate
activity, and expert occupancy determine which instructions actually execute.

## 🔍 Aichen NVFP4 transfer points

The checked-out MXFP4 body and Aichen NVFP4 body use the same high-level SM90
resource plan:

- `kNumDispatchRegisters=48`
- `kNumEpilogueRegisters=208`
- a compile-time aggregate register budget of `64512`
- `BLOCK_M=8` with four stages, `BLOCK_M=16/24` with three stages,
  `BLOCK_M=64` with three stages, and `BLOCK_M=128` with six stages

The reusable lesson is the protocol rather than a blind source transplant.
Hopper WGMMA is an asynchronous warpgroup operation whose fence, commit, and
wait ordering must remain intact.[^3] Likewise, independent mbarrier objects
may be initialized by different lanes, but all initialization must be visible
before any producer or consumer uses those objects.[^3]

## 🎯 CAKE optimization hypotheses

1. Preserve the current candidate that stripes independent barrier
   initialization across lanes. It targets launch-fixed latency and is most
   likely to help `M=8..64`; validate that `SYNCS.EXCH` initialization and the
   publication fence retain their original ordering.
2. Treat `BN=256` as the preferred wide-MMA path when expert occupancy permits.
   Its static body has fewer LDS/STS, SYNCS, and WARPGROUP operations than the
   `BN=128` specialization.
3. Do not tune only the small-M rows. Promotion still requires correctness at
   `M=8,32` and paired FP8-versus-MXFP4 measurements for all eleven Flash rows
   through `M=8192`.
4. Once an eight-GPU H20 node is idle, compile each promoted candidate and
   capture its own resource usage and SASS. Compare candidate versus incumbent
   with identical specialization parameters; do not compare unlike template
   variants.
